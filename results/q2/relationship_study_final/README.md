# Q2 final relationship study outputs

Generated from the frozen Q2.1 six-curve output. This is descriptive relationship and effective-lag analysis; no prediction models are trained or evaluated.

Versioned compact evidence:
- lag_summary.csv: window-scale lag distributions and BH diagnostic counts.
- flow_delay_summary.csv: low/middle/high oxygen-flow lag medians.
- within_heat_flow_contrast_summary.csv: within-heat high-vs-low flow lag contrasts.
- stage_adjusted_lag_slope_summary.csv: within-heat flow-lag slopes before and after process-stage adjustment.
- block_relationship_summary.csv: 30-second increment associations across the ten heats.
- stage_relationship_summary.csv: stage-wise gas-flow and marginal-emission summaries under H0/H1.
- manifest.json: input fingerprint and analysis settings.

Full-resolution lag windows, dose-response curves, per-heat outputs and the complete set of intermediate tables are regenerated by the experiment script and are not duplicated here.

The analysis does not find robust evidence that higher lance flow yields shorter gas-signal delays. H0 is the main timebase; H1 is sensitivity only.
