# Q2.2 V4 — Analytic oxygen-flow reconstruction

## Purpose

Replace finite-difference oxygen-flow estimates with the analytic derivative of a fitted cumulative-oxygen curve. The result is a smooth, nonnegative **effective instantaneous-flow estimate**, not an independently observed sensor truth.

## Model

Let the instantaneous oxygen flow be a cubic B-spline:

\[
q(t)=\sum_{j=1}^{p} c_j B_j(t), \qquad c_j\geq 0.
\]

The fitted cumulative oxygen is its analytic integral:

\[
\widehat O(t)=O(0)+\int_0^t q(u)\,du.
\]

Because cubic B-spline basis functions are nonnegative on the fitted domain and all coefficients are constrained nonnegative, the estimated flow is nonnegative by construction. The derivative is evaluated directly as `B(t) @ coef`; no numerical differencing of the fitted cumulative curve is used.

The coefficient fit minimizes a robust weighted cumulative-counter residual plus a second-difference penalty. Huber-style iteratively reweighted least squares limits the influence of isolated counter irregularities.

## Parameter selection and validation

- Knot spacing: 5, 10, or 20 seconds.
- Second-difference penalty: 0.1, 1, 10, or 100.
- Final reconstruction: four contiguous holdout blocks (blocks 1, 3, 4, and 6 in zero-based indexing) select the minimum-CV-MAE setting.
- Sensitivity band: refit all settings within one standard error of the best CV MAE; the 10th–90th percentile of these curves is a **hyperparameter sensitivity envelope**, not a confidence interval.
- `flow_nested_block_validation.csv` preserves the previously executed nested within-heat validation: outer blocks 2 and 5 held out, inner blocks 1, 3, 4, and 6 used for parameter selection. It compares the integral spline with linear and PCHIP cumulative interpolation.

The cumulative holdout metric is MAE/RMSE in m³. It validates interpolation of the cumulative counter over short missing blocks; it does not directly validate pointwise instantaneous flow.

## Timebase sensitivity

Both scenarios remain in the results:

- H0: 0.5 seconds per row for all ten heats.
- H1: 1.0 second per row for heat `6C02057` only; 0.5 seconds for the other heats.

For the fifth heat, the same cumulative increments imply approximately twice the average flow under H0 as under H1. The timebase remains unresolved and neither scenario is promoted as ground truth.

## Files

- `flow_profile.csv`: raw/fitted cumulative oxygen, analytic derivative, one-SE sensitivity envelope, and observed 10/30/60-second secant-rate references. This row-level file is generated by the script but intentionally not versioned because of its size.
- `flow_summary.csv`: per-heat flow and mass-balance summaries.
- `flow_cv_grid.csv`: final four-fold CV scores for every parameter pair.
- `flow_one_se_sensitivity_set.csv`: settings used to construct the sensitivity envelope.
- `flow_nested_block_validation.csv`, `flow_nested_block_summary.csv`: nested outer-block comparison with linear/PCHIP baselines.
Diagnostic PNG figures were generated for review but are kept as local analysis artifacts rather than versioned result files.

## Interpretation limits

There is no independent ground truth for instantaneous oxygen flow between counter updates. The analytic derivative is therefore a physically constrained, smoothed effective-flow reconstruction. Secant rates are descriptive comparisons only. The one-SE envelope reflects parameter sensitivity, not sampling uncertainty. Across all 20 heat/scenario fits, flow remained nonnegative, fitted cumulative oxygen remained monotone, and the maximum trapezoidal mass-balance discrepancy was 0.0773 m³ (`flow_validation_checks.csv`). The counter's timebase and the fifth heat's H0/H1 ambiguity remain material limitations.
