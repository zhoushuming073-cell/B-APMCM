# Q3 Stage-Aware Prediction V1

Exploratory study of stage-conditioned spectral/gas delay and regime-specific Ridge experts.

## Data and execution

- Official corrected Data.zip; SHA-256: cb662ce5ea17c247d0733ec8a4245fec37abf82d823a0ddc08597b300d95a727.
- Ten heats, 13,589 rows, 2,047 native wavelength channels.
- H0 only: 0.5 s per row.
- Run from the repository root:

~~~bash
PYTHONPATH=src python -m apmcm.q3.stage_aware \
  --data-zip data/raw/official/Data.zip \
  --output-dir results/q3/stage_aware_v1
~~~

The launcher executes the archived source bundle src/apmcm/q3/stage_aware_source.py.gz. To inspect an uncompressed copy:

~~~python
import gzip
from pathlib import Path
src = Path("src/apmcm/q3/stage_aware_source.py.gz")
Path("src/apmcm/q3/stage_aware_source.py").write_bytes(gzip.decompress(src.read_bytes()))
~~~

Dependencies are covered by the repository lock file: numpy, pandas, scipy, scikit-learn and pyxlsb.

## Methods

1. Four gas-defined stages are created by a fixed four-segment diagnostic fit to log total CO+CO2 flow and logit apparent CO2 share. These are retrospective pseudo-labels, not physical ground truth.
2. Stage-specific lags are estimated using training heats only. A held-out heat is routed using training-only median elapsed-time or cumulative-oxygen thresholds.
3. The stage-lag global Ridge and stage-specific Ridge experts are compared with a global-lag/global-Ridge baseline.
4. Outer evaluation is leave-one-heat-out. Inner leave-one-heat-out folds select Ridge regularization. Standardization, PCA, lag selection, routing thresholds and model coefficients are fit without the held-out heat.
5. Tail slopes and boundary perturbations are reported as diagnostics, not optimization targets.

## Main findings

- Stage-conditioned lags vary: median CO2 offsets are 15/25/20/35 s across stages; CO offsets are 0/25/20/35 s. The stage-4 correlation is weak.
- With elapsed-time routing, the stage-expert model improves CO2 macro R2 from 0.134 to 0.569 and MAE from 2.934 to 2.123 on common support.
- CO improves only modestly: macro R2 0.709 to 0.712; MAE 6.378 to 5.973.
- Spectral features do not improve four-stage classification over the elapsed-time baseline.
- The model still misses the CO2 terminal reversal in 9/10 heats.

## Limitations

- Ten heats have been repeatedly explored; LOHO is not independent confirmation.
- Stage labels are retrospective gas-derived diagnostics.
- The stage-4 lag peak is weak and must not be interpreted as a stable transport delay.
- Time/oxygen routing is not equivalent to spectral hidden-state recognition.
- The stage-expert model does not solve the CO2 terminal reversal and is not a deployable final model.
- Do not compare its R2 directly with historical V5 or boosted models unless the information set, prediction lead, score domain and outer folds are made identical.
