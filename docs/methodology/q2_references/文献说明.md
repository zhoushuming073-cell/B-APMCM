# 参考文献及用途

1. Fritsch, F. N. & Butland, J. (1984). A Method for Constructing Local Monotone Piecewise Cubic Interpolants. *SIAM Journal on Scientific and Statistical Computing*, 5(2), 300–304. 烟气节点间保形三次插值及耗氧重构的对照方法。节点宽度、异常判据和时滞模型是本实验设定。https://doi.org/10.1137/0905021
2. Roberts, D. R., et al. (2017). Cross-validation strategies for data with temporal, spatial, hierarchical, or phylogenetic structure. *Ecography*, 40, 913–929. 对具有组内依赖的数据按独立组验证。本文把炉次作为外层验证单位。https://doi.org/10.1111/ecog.02881
3. Bathla, R. (2011). Carbon Prediction Model Using the Offgas Analysis at ArcelorMittal Indiana Harbor No. 2 Steel Producing BOF Shop with No Bottom Blowing and Suppressed Hood Combustion System. *Iron & Steel Technology*. 该厂区分了烟道输运、取样管与仪器响应。其工况和管路与赛题设备未对齐，现场报告的秒数没有参与本实验标定。https://www.researchgate.net/publication/293360540
4. The Pennsylvania State University. *STAT 510, Lesson 9.1, Pre-whitening as an Aid to Interpreting the CCF*. 说明自相关、共同趋势对互相关的干扰。本研究做平滑变化率诊断和循环错位对照，没有拟合完整的 ARIMA 预白化模型。https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat510/Lesson09
